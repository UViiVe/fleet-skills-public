# Grant Section Briefing Template

For writing Significance, Background, or Innovation sections of grant applications.

---

## Briefing

**Grant mechanism:** [R01 / R21 / K-award / foundation grant / etc.]
**Section:** [Significance / Background / Innovation / Approach]
**Target disease/gene/topic:** [NAME]
**Funding agency priorities:** [NIH study section focus / foundation mandate / etc.]
**Word/page limit:** [N words or N pages]

**⚠️ VERIFICATION WARNING:**
Verify every PMID by direct fetch from https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/[PMID]/.
Do NOT fabricate PMIDs or statistics. Every prevalence figure, mortality statistic,
and clinical claim must trace to a verified citation.

---

## Task: Grant [SECTION] for [TOPIC]

Write a [SECTION] section for a [MECHANISM] grant application on [TOPIC].

**Required elements:**
- Establish the clinical/scientific significance (burden of disease, unmet need)
- Summarize current state of knowledge (verified citations)
- Identify the critical gap this proposal addresses
- State why now (recent advances that make the proposed work timely)
- [For Innovation:] Distinguish from prior approaches

**Tone:** Scientific, precise, persuasive. Written for expert reviewers.
**Citation density:** High — every major claim cited.
**Format:** Flowing prose with in-text citations [PMID: XXXXXXXX].
