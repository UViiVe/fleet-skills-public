# Safety and scientific scope

A successful call establishes technical access only. A prediction is not an
experiment, pathogenicity classification, clinical test or treatment recommendation.
Observed labels describe training-data availability, not measured values for the
user's sample. Imputed exploratory channels deserve particular caution.

The service is offered for noncommercial research under its own terms. Review
https://alphagenome.google/api before using it. The MIT integration license grants
no service entitlement, commercial permission or rights in upstream materials.

Human and mouse support do not establish zebrafish generalization. The CLI cannot
upload cell matrices or sequences. For separately approved expression conditioning,
review the official human-data tutorial and consent/data-transfer agreements first.
API keys are credentials: use an environment variable, do not share a key, and
rotate through the provider if accidentally disclosed. Never log full request/error
objects that may contain credentials or biological inputs.
